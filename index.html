Real data, credited

Public datasets with their licenses noted. Where real data can't exist publicly, such as a retailer's settlements or budget, it's generated and labeled as such.

Tested, not just run

Every project has automated tests and CI. Results in the READMEs are generated by the code, not typed in.

Caught and fixed

Each project documents something that went wrong: a label leak, a silent spreadsheet bug, a model that lost to a simpler one.

Honest limits

What wasn't tested, what's simulated, and what I'd do next is written down in each repository.
